The Team

Three attorneys. One standard of care.

Every matter at Edmond Law is handled by an attorney who cares about the outcome as much as you do. Meet the people who will stand in your corner.

The attorneys at Edmond Law bring together decades of combined experience across Jamaican law, and an equal commitment to the clients they serve. We do not hand cases to clerks. When you engage Edmond Law, you work directly with a qualified attorney from first consultation to final resolution.

Founding Partner

Nicholas M.D. Edmond

Attorney-at-Law

Nicholas Edmond co-founded Edmond Law with the conviction that great legal service should not be the preserve of those who can afford a Kingston firm. With a background spanning conveyancing, civil litigation, and commercial law, he brings a breadth of expertise to a practice that is anything but ordinary.

Called to the Jamaican Bar, Nicholas has represented clients in the Resident Magistrates' Court, the Supreme Court of Jamaica, and the Court of Appeal. His practice is characterised by thorough preparation, measured advocacy, and a commitment to reaching the best achievable outcome, not merely the fastest one.

When he is not in chambers or in court, Nicholas is a committed advocate for greater legal literacy in the communities Edmond Law serves. He believes that a well-informed client is a better-protected one, and brings that ethos to every consultation.

Areas of focus: Conveyancing & Real Estate · Civil Litigation · Commercial Contracts · Probate & Administration

Founding Partner

Tamica N. Edmond

Attorney-at-Law

Tamica N. Edmond is the operational and relational heart of Edmond Law. As the principal point of contact for the firm's clients, she brings exceptional care to every interaction, ensuring that clients feel heard, supported, and properly advised at every stage of their matter.

Tamica's practice encompasses family law, estate planning, and personal injury, areas that require not only legal rigour but genuine human sensitivity. Her clients often say she made a difficult process feel manageable. That is the result of years of deliberate, client-centred practice.

She is particularly committed to the firm's family law practice, where she has guided clients through divorce, custody, and property matters with the discretion and determination those situations demand. Tamica is also the firm's primary contact for all enquiries: tamica@edmondlaw.org or 876-827-3362.

Areas of focus: Family Law · Estate Planning · Personal Injury · Probate & Administration

Attorney-at-Law

Michelle A. Daley

Attorney-at-Law

Michelle A. Daley joined Edmond Law because she believed in what the firm stands for: accessible, rigorous, respectful legal representation for Jamaicans. She has been a valued member of the team ever since, bringing sharp analytical thinking and a strong work ethic to every matter she handles.

Michelle's practice focuses on criminal defence, civil litigation, and labour law, areas where the stakes are often high and the margin for error is slim. She approaches every case with the preparation it deserves, never underestimating an opponent and never treating a client's matter as routine.

Her background in labour and employment law has become a growing part of her practice, as more Jamaican employees and employers recognise the value of having proper legal representation in workplace disputes. Michelle is committed to ensuring that those who come to Edmond Law receive the best possible result, whatever the forum.

Areas of focus: Civil & Criminal Litigation · Labour & Employment Law · Contracts
