About the Firm

A Jamaican firm built on responsiveness, rigour, and respect.

Edmond Law is a full-service practice serving clients across Jamaica from our offices in Portmore, St. Catherine. We combine the technical depth of a traditional chambers with the accessibility of a modern firm that answers messages, returns calls, and explains the law in language you can use.

Whether you're closing on property, settling an estate, or navigating a difficult chapter, we treat your matter with the seriousness it deserves and the warmth you remember.

Our Difference

Why clients choose Edmond Law.

Three principles that guide everything we do, from the first call to the final resolution.

Responsive

We return calls. We answer messages. When you reach out, you hear back from an attorney who knows your file, not a receptionist. You'll never feel like a ticket number here.

Rigorous

Every matter we accept receives thorough legal analysis. We don't cut corners on research, and we don't sign documents we haven't read. Your matter deserves our full attention, and that is what it gets.

Respectful

We explain the law in plain language, treat every client's matter with genuine care, and maintain the strictest confidentiality. Legal proceedings can be stressful. We work to make them less so.
