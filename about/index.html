Our Story

A firm built on conviction and still guided by it.

Since opening our doors in Portmore, Edmond Law has been guided by one principle: that every client, regardless of the complexity of their matter, deserves the same standard of care we would want for ourselves.

Our Story

From Portmore to clients across Jamaica.

Edmond Law was founded by Nicholas and Tamica Edmond with a clear-eyed purpose: to build a law firm that Jamaicans could actually access, one that answered the phone, spoke plainly, and stayed in their corner through every turn of their matter.

Portmore was the obvious home. A growing, vibrant community that had long been underserved by legal practitioners willing to invest in it. From our offices in Big Buy Plaza, we have assisted hundreds of Jamaican families and businesses navigate some of the most consequential moments of their lives: the purchase of a first home, the grief of settling a parent's estate, and the resolve required to stand in a courtroom and fight for what is right.

We have grown deliberately. Each attorney we have added to the firm, including Michelle Daley, was chosen not merely for their technical competence but for their character. We believe clients can feel the difference between an attorney who is merely qualified and one who genuinely cares. Our clients deserve the latter, and that is what we hire.

Today, Edmond Law offers a full range of legal services to individuals, families, and businesses across all fourteen parishes. The firm remains privately held, personally led, and deeply committed to the promise printed on our letterhead: Experience service with a difference.

What We Stand For

Four values that guide every decision we make.

These are not words on a wall. They are principles that inform how we think, how we advise, and how we show up for every client.

Integrity

We give honest advice, even when it isn't what a client hopes to hear. Building trust requires telling the truth, and we will never compromise on that.

Accessibility

Legal counsel should not be a luxury. We price our services fairly, explain the law clearly, and make ourselves available when our clients need us most.

Excellence

Every document we draft, every submission we file, every argument we make is prepared to the highest standard we can deliver. Good enough is not our benchmark.

Discretion

What you share with us stays with us. Confidentiality is not just a professional obligation. It is the foundation of the trust our clients place in us.

How We Work

Listen. Plan. Execute.

Every matter follows the same disciplined process because your outcome depends on it.

01

Listen

We begin every engagement by listening properly. We take time to understand your situation, your concerns, and your goals before we offer any advice. Good counsel starts with good listening.

02

Plan

Once we understand your matter, we map a clear path forward. You will know our strategy, the likely timeline, the costs involved, and the risks we need to manage. No surprises.

03

Execute

We pursue your matter with the rigour it demands, filing on time, following up relentlessly, and keeping you informed at every material step until the matter is resolved.

Our Community

Rooted in Portmore. Serving all of Jamaica.

Portmore is one of the most densely populated municipalities in the Caribbean, and for many years legal services here did not reflect the size or sophistication of the community. Edmond Law set out to change that and become the firm that Portmore residents could walk into, trust, and leave feeling well served.

That local commitment has never limited our reach. We represent clients in Kingston, Spanish Town, and across all fourteen parishes. Whether your matter is down the road or on the other side of the island, the standard of care you receive from Edmond Law remains the same.
